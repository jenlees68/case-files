Privacy Policy

Case Files Detective, by Rexakinz Apps · Last updated October 21, 2026

Case Files Detective is a learning game for kids. We built it for families, so we collect as little as possible. There are no ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases, and no tracking or analytics tools.

What stays on the phone

Game progress is saved only on the device where the game is played. That includes each kid's first name or nickname, chosen avatar and pet name, levels, scores, badges, reward points, the rewards list a grown-up sets up, and settings. The grown-up PIN is stored in scrambled (hashed) form.

The microphone

A grown-up decides whether the game may use the microphone. If they approve it, the microphone is used only while a reading page is open, so the game can check words a kid reads out loud. The phone's built-in speech recognition service (on Android, provided by Google) turns the speech into text. Case Files Detective does not record, save or send any audio.

The optional progress page

A grown-up can choose to link the game to a progress page to check progress from another device. Only if they do this, a copy of the game progress described above (never the PIN) is sent to our database, hosted by Supabase in the United States. It is protected by secret keys that only the grown-up's page and the linked phone hold. No email address, phone number, location or contact information is collected.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share any information with anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.

Deleting data

  • In the game: Grown-ups → Family & help → Erase all game data erases all Case Files data on the phone. Nothing else on the phone is affected.
  • Removing a single kid from Family & help erases that kid's data.
  • Uninstalling the app also erases everything stored on the phone.
  • If you linked a progress page, email us at rexakinzapps@gmail.com and we'll delete the online copy.

Kids

The game is designed for children and their grown-ups. Grown-up settings, links that leave the app, and email are behind a PIN. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the first name or nickname a grown-up enters.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: rexakinzapps@gmail.com

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
